5. **Chinese (*Lo Shu* / Bagua):** 3x3 Magic Square harmonics and linguistic homophones.

### What the Classical Six Cannot Do

Despite their historical nuance, all legacy systems suffer from three mathematical and structural flaws:

1. **The Entropy/Reduction Trap:** Classical systems sum digits recursively until they collapse into a single scalar ($1\text{--}9$, plus master numbers). 2. **Static Snapshots vs. Dynamic Evolution:** Classical systems treat identity as a permanent, rigid number. They fail to explain how a person behaves under stress, how they function in contemplative rest, or how their energetic orientation shifts as they age through time.
3. **Arbitrary Alphabetical Bias:** Pythagorean relies entirely on the modern Latin alphabet order; Semitic systems require rigid Semitic roots. None transfer cleanly across multiple languages or modern digital systems without distorted transliterations.
